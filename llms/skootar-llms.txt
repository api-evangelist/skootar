# Skootar

> Skootar is a Thai on-demand courier, messenger, and logistics platform serving Bangkok, its surrounding provinces, and Chonburi. It handles same-day document delivery, cheque/bill collection, parcel shipping, business food delivery, and on-demand truck/moving jobs across a fleet of motorcycles, cars, and trucks, with real-time tracking. Skootar reports 130,000+ business customers, 26,000+ drivers, and 9.2M+ completed deliveries over 12 years, and is backed by 500 Global.

Orders can be placed 24/7 via the website, mobile app, or an API intended for high-volume businesses to connect delivery ordering into their own back-office systems. Skootar publishes no public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or self-service API documentation as of this writing; API access is arranged through its business/enterprise sales channel.
